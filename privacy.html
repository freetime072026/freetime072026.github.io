Privacy Policy — Purrge!

Effective date: October 19, 2026

This privacy policy describes how FreeTime ("we", "us") handles information in the mobile game Purrge! (Android package com.freetime.basetd, the "App").

Summary

  • The App does not show advertising and does not use an advertising ID.
  • The App has no in-app purchases.
  • The App has no account or login.
  • The App does not ask for your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos or other personal files.
  • We collect limited, pseudonymous technical data to run the game, fix crashes and understand gameplay, using Google Firebase.
  • Your game progress is stored on your device.

Information we collect

DataPurposeService
App instance ID Count active installations anonymously (the App has no account and no sign-in) Google Analytics for Firebase
Gameplay events (e.g. stage started / finished, waves cleared, settings changed), session length Analytics — understand how the game is played and improve balance Google Analytics for Firebase
Crash logs, stack traces, device model, OS version, app version, free memory / disk at crash time Diagnose and fix crashes Firebase Crashlytics
Device model, OS version, language, app version, approximate country derived from IP address Deliver game configuration values Firebase Remote Config

This data is not linked to your real-world identity. We do not sell it and do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. It is transmitted encrypted (HTTPS).

Your choices (consent)

On first launch the App asks for your consent to analytics. You can change it at any time in Settings ▸ Privacy inside the App. If you decline, analytics events are not sent; crash reports may still be sent to keep the game stable.

Data stored on your device

Game progress (stages cleared, currencies, settings, player name you type in-game) and a random player ID are saved locally on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data.

Third-party services

The App uses the following Google services, each with its own privacy policy:

  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config)
  • Google Privacy Policy

Data retention

  • Analytics data: kept for up to 14 months (Google Analytics retention setting), then deleted automatically.
  • Crash reports: kept for up to 90 days.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the App stops all collection and deletes local game data. To ask us to delete data associated with your installation, email freetime.07.2026@gmail.com with the subject "Purrge! data deletion" and include the player ID shown in Settings (tap to copy). We will process the request within 30 days.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK or California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of your personal data, to withdraw consent, and to object. Contact us at the email below to exercise these rights. We do not sell or "share" personal information as defined by the CCPA/CPRA.

International transfers

Google may process data on servers outside your country. Google uses legally recognized safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version; significant changes will be announced in the App or on its store page.
